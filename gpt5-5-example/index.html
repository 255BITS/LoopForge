Design Name

Bloodrift: Star-Crypt Survivors

A one-file browser prototype blending three fantasies: the frictionless survival dodging of Asteroids, the automatic buildcraft chaos of survivor-likes, and the dark loot-hunt atmosphere of a gothic action RPG. The late game now uses object caps, spatial collision bins, and automatic overflow harvesting so the chaos keeps moving without turning into a slideshow.

Prototype Loop

Vampire Survivors Core Weapons auto-cast, while held-mouse blood lances let you steer burst damage into priority targets.
Asteroids Core Momentum, drift, screen wrap, safe toroidal targeting, splitting bone-meteors, dash parries, and boost windows.
Diablo Feel Named bosses, cursed caches, elites, blessing fountains, Heat-fueled Bloodfrenzy, and greedy gold healing.
